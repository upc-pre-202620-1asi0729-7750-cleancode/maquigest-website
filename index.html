About us

CleanCode, the team behind MaquiGest

CleanCode is a startup focused on developing accessible digital solutions to organize and optimize processes for small and medium-sized businesses. Its approach focuses on solving operational problems through specialized tools that are easy to use and tailored to its users' needs.

Our mission

Our mission is to help small and medium-sized construction machinery and equipment rental businesses manage all their operations through a simple, accessible and reliable digital platform. We aim to centralize their operations, reduce errors related to availability and reservations, improve control over equipment condition and provide a better experience for both businesses and people who rent machinery.

Our vision

Our vision is to become a leading startup in Peru in digital solutions for construction equipment rental management, helping small and medium-sized businesses professionalize their operations and provide more efficient, organized and reliable services.

Our values

  • Innovation: We apply technology to improve and simplify traditional equipment rental processes.
  • Simplicity: We design solutions that are easy to understand and accessible to businesses with different levels of technological experience.
  • Responsibility: We promote proper management of equipment, information and rental operations.
  • Collaboration: We value teamwork and communication with the businesses and people who will use MaquiGest.
  • Quality: We strive to offer a reliable, organized platform focused on the real needs of its users.
